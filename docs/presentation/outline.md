## 2. The question: what remains unsolved after the modulator revolution?

**On slide**

Where do meaningful unmet needs remain, how well is the current ecosystem addressing them, and which areas appear most promising for future innovation or company formation?

**Say**

Highly effective modulators changed survival, lung function, infection, and transplant volume. The remaining question is who is still left out, what disease persists on treatment, and where the pipeline is crowded versus thin.

## 5. Current therapeutic landscape

**On slide**

Solved classes: CFTR modulators for protein-producing genotypes · PERT · inhaled antibiotics · dornase / hypertonic saline · insulin for CFRD

Not solved as products: mutation-agnostic CFTR restoration · CF-specific metabolic disease-modifier · disease-modifying CFLD · novel persistent-infection modalities beyond inhaled SoC

**Say**

Standard of care occupying a job is not white space. Residual disease on that standard of care might be.

## 8. Opportunity scoring framework

**On slide**

Score = 20 × Σ(wᵢ × sᵢ) · sᵢ on 1–5 · display integer /100 plus Evidence: High / Moderate / Low

Default weights: need 25 · gap 20 · whitespace 20 · tractability 15 · economic 10 · why now 10

Evidence is never averaged in. Economic relevance is not a TAM. Theses inherit the parent need score.

Show the live sliders in the app if presenting from a laptop. Patient-impact weights put N1 above N5. Top-three membership stays N5, N1, N4.

## 10. Deep dive: H1 CFRD / metabolic

**On slide**

Opportunity Score: 77/100 · Evidence: Moderate · Pursue diligence

FACT. 19.2% / 29.3% adults · 16.4% no treatment noted · ~58% CGM · no CF-specific disease-modifying metabolic approval

ANALYSIS. Reviewed industry drug map empty

BEAR. Generics plus insulin may be enough. 16.4% untreated can be a care gap. Ranking first is a whitespace result.

KILL. Academic SGLT2/GLP-1 close the gap, or HEMT-era incidence collapses.

**Say**

Do not treat 77 as a mandate to form a company. Diligence the academic readouts before a CF-specific mechanism.

## 11. What would need to be true?

**On slide**

H1. CFRD stays prevalent on HEMT; off-the-shelf metabolic drugs are insufficient; a CF-specific endpoint is feasible.

H2. ABCI-class ionophores work chronically in no-protein genotypes; nucleic-acid programs do not cover them first.

H3. Persistent PA or NTM still drives residual lung disease; phage or a peer can be manufactured and regulated.

H5. 861 people with cirrhosis and a portal-hypertension endpoint can support a development path.

**Say**

If those statements fail, the card dies. That is the point of the memo.
