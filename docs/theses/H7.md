Thirteen industry investigational crowding units sit on N1: 4D-710, ARCT-032, RCT2100, VX-522, KB407, SP-101, SPL84, BI 3720931, plus related preclinical listings that remained map-eligible after review. Vertex is in the mRNA race. N1 scores 72/100 default (whitespace 1/5) and 79/100 on the patient-impact preset. Evidence is High.

## INTERPRETATION

This is the cleanest remaining CFTR-function hole and the worst default newco. Ineligible count is not a TAM: many of the 2,434 are ineligible by age, not by a treatable no-protein genotype. Historical readthrough (ataluren) failed.

## Bull case

Biology and endpoints are clear. Patient-impact weights put N1 first. A differentiated delivery or editing approach could still matter if current programs fail.

## Bear case

A me-too newco adds a 14th program, not a missing mechanism. Lung delivery, not target identification, is the bottleneck. ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING VX-522 is still treated as active_clinical. CFF past-tense preclinical listings were taken off the map for a reason.

## Counterarguments

- Ineligible count is not a TAM; use the 1,226 adolescents/adults with Registry data, not 2,434, when discussing treatable genotypes.
- Do not narrate VX-522 as a vacuum because it is not recruiting.
- ABCI (H2) is the orthogonal N1 hedge; another inhaled CFTR mRNA is not.

## What would have to be true

A new delivery or editing approach is differentiated from 4D-710 VX-522 ARCT-032 RCT2100 KB407 SP-101 SPL84 and related listings; ineligible genotypes still lack restoration after those programs read out.

## Kill criteria

Kill as a company-formation default if current clinical nucleic-acid programs show meaningful restoration. Keep as a watch-incumbents card not a me-too newco.

## Remaining diligence

- Wait for human restoration signals from the current clinical set before company-formation.
- If forming anything, it must be a differentiated delivery or editing approach, not another inhaled CFTR mRNA.
- Keep ABCI (H2) as the orthogonal N1 hedge rather than a second nucleic-acid vehicle.

## Verdict
