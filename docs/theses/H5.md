## The claim (HYPOTHESIS)

PERT is a solved product class. Advanced CFLD is small, severe, and untreated by a disease-modifying drug.

PERT is a solved product class; advanced CFLD is small severe and untreated by a disease-modifying drug.

## FACT

PERT use is 80.2%. Cirrhosis is 2.7% of the complications population (861 people). CFF recommends against routine UDCA to prevent advanced CFLD; UDCA was still prescribed in 10.1%. No approved disease-modifying CFLD therapy is in the MVP table.

## ANALYSIS

N6 industry crowding is moderate (two units): ANG003 and NHS7108 are GI/lipase candidates, not liver disease-modifiers. The need scores 60/100 with evidence Moderate because the category mixes a solved enzyme class with a rare severe liver problem.

## INTERPRETATION

The interesting remaining gap is CFLD, not another pancreatic enzyme. 861 people with cirrhosis is a hard biotech n. Older-adult PERT decline is interpreted by CFF as milder surviving phenotypes, not solved pancreatic disease.

## Bull case

A disease-modifying CFLD path would not compete with PERT incumbents. Negative UDCA guidance leaves a documented pharmacologic hole if endpoints can be defined.

## Bear case

Portal-hypertension endpoints are slow and noisy. A lipase newco is incremental to an existing class. Liver-disease development in other rare diseases has long timelines that a CF-only vehicle may not support.

## Counterarguments

- PERT whitespace is not the interesting claim; CFLD is, and CFLD may be too small.
- Industry GI crowding is about enzymes and nutrition, not cirrhosis.
- Transplant-censored tables may hide post-transplant liver disease and shrink the countable group further.

## What would have to be true

Cirrhosis/portal-hypertension endpoints can be studied; the 861-person cirrhosis group is large enough for a development path; UDCA-negative guidance leaves a real gap.

## Kill criteria

Kill if the addressable advanced-CFLD population is too small for a trial or if a feasible endpoint cannot be defined.

## Remaining diligence

- Split N6 in any later scoring: PERT vs CFLD should not share one venture thesis.
- Confirm whether 861 is incident enough for a trial.
- Look for non-CF cholestatic or fibrotic analogs only as biology, not as a TAM overlay.

## Verdict

Pursue diligence on CFLD endpoints and n, not on another PERT. Kill if the addressable advanced-liver group cannot support a trial.
