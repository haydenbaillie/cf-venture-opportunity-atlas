## The claim (HYPOTHESIS)

Inhaled antibiotics remain SoC; novel CF infection programs are thin after review.

Approved inhaled antibiotics remain SoC; novel CF infection programs are thin after review.

## FACT

PA is 23.3% of cultured patients (52.0% in 2009). NTM is 11.6% of mycobacterial cultures and 18.7% among ages 60+. Inhaled tobramycin remains 51.2% of PA-positive ages ≥6; inhaled aztreonam 30.3%. Fewer cultures since 2020 affect ascertainment.

## ANALYSIS

After review, industry investigational crowding on N4 is low: BiomX BX004. Academic phage and IV gallium are visible. AP-PA02 had no matching active CF-like trial in this snapshot. N4 scores 66/100 with evidence Moderate.

## INTERPRETATION

SoC occupies chronic suppression. The remaining question is persistent PA or NTM on that SoC, not an untreated infection market. Falling PA is real and partly measurement-sensitive.

## Bull case

Approved inhaled antibiotics are still widely used and novel CF infection programs are thin. A manufacturable, regulated modality against persistent PA or NTM would sit in a reviewed industry column that currently has one crowding unit.

## Bear case

If BX004 works, it becomes the incumbent rather than leaving a hole. If PA keeps falling on HEMT, the addressable chronic-infection group shrinks. Phage CMC, resistance, and regulation are messy. NTM in older adults may be aging and ascertainment more than a CF-specific phage story.

## Counterarguments

- SoC exists; this is residual disease on treatment, not an untreated infection market.
- Academic phage protocols can look like activity without being company-formation infrastructure.
- Public Registry tables do not cleanly separate chronic from intermittent PA.

## What would have to be true

Chronic PA or NTM still drives residual lung disease on HEMT; phage or another modality can be manufactured and regulated; culture-ascertainment decline is not the whole PA story.

## Kill criteria

Kill if BX004 or similar phage succeeds as a broad incumbent or if PA/NTM prevalence and inhaled-antibiotic use keep collapsing without a new product.

## Remaining diligence

- Separate chronic vs intermittent PA before sizing a product.
- Watch BX004 readout before starting a second PA phage newco.
- Treat NTM as a distinct development problem from PA, not one infection TAM.

## Verdict

Pursue diligence on a defined persistent-infection segment. Do not pitch 'CF infection' as empty.
