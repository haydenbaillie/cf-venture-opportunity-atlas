### N2 — Eligible but not prescribed

Default score 46/100.

The 623 people aged 12+ who were eligible in 2020 and had no modulator prescription in 2022–2024 are a mixed group (side effects, perceived stability, access). Next-generation Vertex modulators already occupy the class. N2 ranks last (46/100) in every weight preset.

- Do not treat 623 as a TAM or as interchangeable with genotype-ineligible.
- Alyftrek pediatric and LTE trials are label expansion, not whitespace.

**Scored and kept in the investigation set. Not a default company-formation card.**

### N7 — Aging with CF

Default score 61/100.

Predicted median survival is 65.4 years; 61.6% of the Registry is adult; 1,437 people are aged 60+. Adult anxiety is 31.9% and depression 30.2%. That is a demographic fact without a single CF-specific molecular target. The reviewed map for N7 is empty because the category is a bundle (bone, cancer screening, mental health, polypharmacy), not a product.

- An empty map here is a taxonomy problem, not proof of a missing drug.
- Do not form a company against 'aging with CF' as one indication.

**Scored and kept in the investigation set. Not a default company-formation card.**
