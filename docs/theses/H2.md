# H2 — CFTR-independent airway anion transport

**Pursue diligence** · N1 · Opportunity Score: 72/100 · Evidence: Moderate

Not medical advice, not a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and not a claim that any opportunity should be funded. Scores are an exploratory index. Theses inherit the parent need score; they are not a second ranking.

## The claim (HYPOTHESIS)

If N1 restoration is crowded, the differentiated bet is CFTR-independent anion transport.

Nucleic-acid restoration is crowded; a bypass mechanism is the differentiated N1 bet.

## FACT

After Alyftrek, CFF estimates ~92% eligible for at least one modulator. 2,434 people were ineligible by age or genotype; 1,226 ineligible adolescents/adults had Registry data. There is no approved mutation-agnostic CFTR restoration.

## ANALYSIS

N1 has 13 industry investigational crowding units (gene therapy, mRNA, ASO). ABCI (amphotericin B cystetic for inhalation, Cystetic Medicines, NCT05802264) is the reviewed, industry, map-eligible Phase 1 ionophore that does not need CFTR protein. Complementary Sionna molecules count as one crowding unit elsewhere on the modulator map, not here.

## INTERPRETATION

A bypass mechanism is orthogonal to the crowded delivery race for nonsense and other no-protein genotypes. One early clinical program is a hedge, not a field. If nucleic-acid restoration works first, bypass becomes backup.

## Bull case

Mutation-agnostic restoration without gene or mRNA delivery. For genotypes that make no CFTR protein, ionophore anion transport is a different job than adding another inhaled CFTR mRNA.

## Bear case

ABCI is early (healthy volunteers and people with CF). Chronic inhaled amphotericin-class ionophore safety, dosing, and airway irritation are the obvious failure modes. Company-formation here is close to a single-asset bet unless a second bypass mechanism appears.

## Counterarguments

- Mutation-agnostic nucleic-acid delivery is the more direct causal fix if it works; bypass is a hedge, not a better biology story.
- Ionophore precedents outside CF do not automatically translate to lifelong inhaled use in damaged airways.
- One recruiting Phase 1 program is not evidence that the class is tractable at chronic doses.

## What would have to be true

ABCI-class ionophores restore airway anion transport without CFTR protein; safety and chronic dosing work in people with CF including nonsense/rare genotypes; nucleic-acid programs do not fully cover the ineligible group first.

## Kill criteria

Kill if ABCI safety/tolerability fails or if a mutation-agnostic mRNA/gene therapy demonstrates clinically meaningful restoration in ineligible genotypes.

## Remaining diligence

- Track ABCI safety, sweat chloride or FEV1 signals, and whether chronic dosing is even attempted.
- Compare genotype coverage against the ineligible 12+ group (1,226 with Registry data), not the full 2,434 ineligible count.
- Do not count ABCI as whitespace if it stalls while remaining listed as recruiting.

## Verdict

Pursue diligence as the differentiated N1 mechanism. It is not safer than nucleic-acid programs; it is less crowded.
